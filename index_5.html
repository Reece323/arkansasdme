Common Questions

Before You Test

What is a Designated Mechanic Examiner?

A DME is someone designated by the FAA to give the oral and practical tests required for your Aviation Maintenance Technician certificate. I evaluate you on the General, Airframe and Powerplant sections and issue your temporary certificate when you pass.

How do I schedule my oral and practical test?

Call or text me at 501-617-1220, or shoot me an email. We'll sort out which sections you're testing, go over your paperwork, and pick a date and place that works for both of us.

What do I need to bring?

Bring a government-issued photo ID, your FAA Form 8610-2 authorization, and your passing written test reports for whichever sections you're testing. I'll tell you about anything else when we set it up.

How long does testing take?

Depends on how many sections you're taking. A full General, Airframe and Powerplant run usually spreads across a few days. I'll map the schedule out with you when you call.

Do you test applicants from outside Arkansas?

I sure do. I get applicants driving in from Missouri, Tennessee, Mississippi and all around the region. Call me and we'll figure out the details.
